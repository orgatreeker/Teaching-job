# Port Blair (Sri Vijaya Puram) — Teacher Jobs, checked 30 Oct 2026

I checked the websites of each school, the government teacher recruitment
site, and local newspaper sites. Note: Port Blair is now officially called
**Sri Vijaya Puram**, so new notices use that name.

## The main finding

Right now, **every open government / KV / Navy school maths job in Port Blair
needs a B.Ed.** With only a B.Sc. you can't apply to these yet.
Your options in Port Blair today are **private schools, coaching centres and
home/online tuition**, all by phone or walk-in (none of them post jobs online).

## 3. Coaching & tuition

- **Aakash Institute, Junglighat** — the only big coaching chain still open
  in Port Blair (Garacharma branch is closed). Ask for "Foundation maths
  faculty (Classes 8–10)" or "doubt-solving faculty".
- Local tuition centres in Aberdeen Bazaar, Junglighat, Dollygunj, Garacharma —
  walk in and ask.
- **Start your own home tuition**: put up a notice ("B.Sc. Maths — Tuition for
  Classes 6–10, CBSE") in your area, at shops and on local Facebook/WhatsApp
  groups. ₹500–1,500 per student per month is typical. This also gives you
  "teaching experience" for your resume.

## 4. The most useful long-term move: B.Ed. in Port Blair

Almost all good teacher jobs here need a B.Ed. **Tagore Government College
of Education (TGCE), Sri Vijaya Puram** runs B.Ed. courses. Watch for admission
notices (usually around May–July) on andamansheekha.com and the TGCE website.
With B.Ed. + CTET, you can apply for KV, Navy School and government TGT Maths
jobs (₹35,000–₹45,000+/month for contract, much more for permanent).
