# Teaching Job Plan — Port Blair → Mumbai

## 1. Your gap

Left college: May 2025. Today: 30 October 2026.
**Gap = 1 year 5 months (17 months).** Not 1.7 years — it's closer to 1.4 years.
This gap is normal and easy to explain: "family responsibilities, now fully available."

## 2. Important fact about teacher jobs in India

| Job type | Needs | Can you apply now? |
|---|---|---|
| Coaching centres / tuition institutes | B.Sc. + good maths | ✅ Yes — best option |
| Online maths tutoring (work from home) | B.Sc. + good English | ✅ Yes |
| Private schools (smaller ones) | B.Sc., B.Ed. preferred | ✅ Some will hire you |
| Big private / international schools | B.Ed. usually required | ⚠️ Hard without B.Ed. |
| Government schools (A&N, KVS, Maharashtra) | B.Ed. + CTET/TET | ❌ Not yet |
| Colleges (lecturer) | M.Sc. + NET/SET | ❌ Not yet |

**Long-term:** start a B.Ed. (2 years, distance/regular) and clear CTET.
That unlocks government jobs and better pay. You can do this while working.

## 3. Step-by-step plan

### Week 1–2 (from home, Port Blair)
1. Fill in `resume.md` with your real details. Make a PDF (Canva or Google Docs).
2. Collect documents: degree, marksheets (10th, 12th, B.Sc.), Aadhaar, PAN,
   4–6 passport photos, bank details. Scan all to your phone/Drive.
3. Create profiles on: **Naukri.com, Indeed, LinkedIn, Apna app, TeacherOn,
   Shine, WorkIndia** — set location to Mumbai + Port Blair.
4. Apply to **online tutoring** (gives income + experience fast):
   Cuemath, Vedantu, PhysicsWallah (Faculty/Doubt-solver roles), Chegg (Expert),
   Filo, Brainly, Superprof, TeacherOn.

### Week 2–4 (Port Blair, in person)
Walk in with resume + documents. Ask for the Principal / Admin office.
- Private schools: Carmel Sr. Sec. School, Vivekananda Kendra Vidyalaya,
  Nirmala School, St. Mary's, Holy Family, Little Flower, other English-medium
  private schools in Port Blair.
- Coaching centres and tuition institutes in Aberdeen Bazaar, Junglighat,
  Garacharma, Dollygunj areas.
- Watch for A&N Administration (Directorate of Education) contract / guest
  teacher notices at **andaman.gov.in** and in the *Andaman Sheekha* / *Daily
  Telegrams* papers.

### Month 1–2 (apply to Mumbai remotely)
Target **20–30 applications per week** on Naukri / Indeed / Apna with keywords:
"Maths teacher", "Mathematics faculty", "Maths tutor", "TGT Maths",
"Foundation maths faculty".
Best targets in Mumbai:
- Coaching chains: Aakash, FIITJEE, Mahesh Tutorials, Chate, Vidyalankar,
  Physics Wallah offline centres, Allen — foundation (Classes 6–10) maths faculty.
- Private English-medium schools in suburbs: Thane, Navi Mumbai, Mira-Bhayandar,
  Kalyan-Dombivli, Vasai-Virar (more likely to hire without B.Ed.).
Ask for **online / video interviews and demo lectures** first.
Practise a 10-minute demo class (e.g. "Pythagoras theorem" or "Linear equations")
and record yourself on phone.

### Month 2–3 (move to Mumbai)
Move **after** you have at least 1 offer or 2–3 interviews fixed.
Stay in a PG/hostel first; take more interviews in person once there.

## 4. Money — how much to carry to Mumbai

| Item | Cost (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Flight Port Blair → Mumbai (book 3–4 weeks early) | ₹7,000 – 12,000 |
| PG / shared room deposit (1–2 months) | ₹8,000 – 20,000 |
| PG rent per month (Thane, Navi Mumbai, Mira Road, Kalyan) | ₹7,000 – 12,000 |
| Food per month (if PG doesn't include) | ₹4,000 – 6,000 |
| Local train pass + phone + misc. per month | ₹1,500 – 3,000 |

**Carry ₹60,000 – 80,000** (covers flight + deposit + 2–3 months living).
Minimum ₹45,000 if you already have a job offer.
Keep money in your bank account; carry only a little cash.

**Expected salary with B.Sc. only:**
coaching ₹18,000–35,000/month · private school ₹12,000–25,000 · online tutoring
₹10,000–30,000 (part-time). With B.Ed. + CTET it goes up a lot.

## 5. Interview answers to prepare

- *"Why the gap?"* → "My parents needed me at home for some time. I kept tutoring
  and studying. That's sorted now and I'm ready to work full-time and relocate."
- *"Why Mumbai?"* → "More opportunities to grow as a teacher, and I'm ready to move."
- *"No B.Ed.?"* → "I'm planning to start B.Ed. and CTET while working."

## 6. Safety tips

- Never pay money to get a job or an interview. Real schools don't charge.
- Check the school/coaching address on Google Maps before travelling.
- Get the offer letter in writing before moving.
