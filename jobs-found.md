## Port Blair — nothing online, so go in person

No Port Blair teacher jobs are posted on Naukri or Indeed. Local jobs here are
filled through notices, newspapers and walk-ins:

- **Kendriya Vidyalaya No. 1 & No. 2, Port Blair** — hire contract maths
  teachers (TGT/PGT) every year, usually Feb–April. Watch
  no1portblair.kvs.ac.in and no2portblair.kvs.ac.in. (Usually need B.Ed.)
- **A&N Directorate of Education** — big teacher drives (380 TGT posts in 2023)
  at edurec.andaman.gov.in. No new maths/TGT drive found for 2026 yet. Needs B.Ed.
- **Navy Children School, Port Blair** — makes a teacher panel each year
  (last one Feb 2024).
- **Private schools & coaching centres** — walk in with your resume
  (Carmel, Vivekananda Kendra Vidyalaya, Nirmala, St. Mary's, coaching in
  Aberdeen Bazaar / Junglighat). Ask if they need a maths teacher or tutor.
- Read the **Andaman Sheekha** and **Daily Telegrams** job notices each week.
